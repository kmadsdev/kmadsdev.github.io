Typography

Display/UI: Rubik (fixed, non-negotiable) — falls back to the system grotesk stack in this self-contained sheet. Metadata: JetBrains Mono / SF Mono stack. UPPERCASE is reserved for eyebrow-class structural labels — never body copy. Stat numerals are tabular-nums.

--t-body · 400

Body copy sits at 1rem/1.6 for long-form reading on the dark canvas. It never shouts, never goes uppercase, and clears AA contrast on every surface it is allowed to sit on.
