# Product

**What:** the personal site of Kaique Souza (Kmads), a backend software engineer.

**Who reads it:** engineers, engineering managers and recruiters who want to see how he thinks and what he built. They read on a laptop first, then on a phone. Many read Portuguese, many read English.

**Its job:** show real backend work in a form a reader can follow in minutes: the problem, the decisions, the result, and how the system runs.

**What is true about it:**
- One page, two languages (English and Brazilian Portuguese), two themes.
- All text lives in `src/content/`. A fact (number, date, link) lives in one file.
- No claim without a source. Internal work of an employer is shown at the level the employer's public material allows.
- No tracking, no cookies, no request to another site for fonts or scripts.

**What it is not:** a blog, a template, or a list of badges.
